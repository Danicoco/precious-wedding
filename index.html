❤️ The Proposal

in her words

He said we had a picnic with a bunch of friends and asked if I could join. I believed him — we had always talked about how a proposal would be: somewhere at a restaurant, eating, and he'd simply give me a ring, I'd look at it and wear it myself. Only this man was keeping a whole surprise for me.

We first arrived at a wrong address, and when he realised and told me, I ran and left him — before someone sent their dogs after us! When we finally got to the right address, he kept telling me to go in first. I said, “In your dreams, baba,” and bluntly refused, so he had no choice but to go in first with someone I'd never met.

I followed only to see rose petals on the floor and balloons everywhere. Then I saw the words on the wall: “Will you marry me?” and this man walked straight to it. I was so shocked I couldn't breathe or move, until he asked me to come closer and began to confess, and asked me to marry him.

What came out of my mouth first was, “Oh my God,” then “Let me think about it,” and then I just said “YES.” It was so beautiful, especially finding out my friends were there too. I was flabbergasted.

Ifeoma
